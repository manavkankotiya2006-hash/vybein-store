A little know-how can make every spritz feel more personal. Here is how fragrance unfolds, where to apply it, and how to care for it.

01 · THE STRUCTURE

The Anatomy of a Fragrance

A fragrance reveals itself in three stages, from the first hello to its lasting impression.

  1. TOPThe first impressionBright notes you notice first.
  2. HEARTThe characterThe central notes that unfold next.
  3. BASEThe lasting trailDeeper notes that linger on skin.
02 · THE RITUAL

The Art of Application

Lightly mist warm pulse points. There is no need to rub.

  1. 1Neck & behind ears
  2. 2Inner wrists
  3. 3Inner elbows
  4. 4Collarbone
03 · THE LONG WEAR

Make Your Fragrance Last

Small habits help your favourite scent stay with you through the day.

  1. 01
    Start with moisturised skin

    Apply after a shower or use an unscented moisturiser first.

  2. 02
    Let it settle

    Spray, then leave the fragrance to dry naturally. Do not rub.

  3. 03
    Store it somewhere cool

    Keep your bottle away from direct sun, heat, and humidity.

  4. 04
    Refresh gently

    A light top-up is usually better than several heavy sprays.
