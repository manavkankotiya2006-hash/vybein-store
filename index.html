Scent school

Wear it with intention.

I

The anatomy

Every extrait unfolds in three movements.

  1. TopThe first impression — bright notes you meet first.
  2. HeartThe character — the notes that unfold next.
  3. BaseThe lasting trail — deep notes that linger on skin.
II

The ritual

Mist lightly on warm pulse points. Never rub.

  1. i.The neck, and just behind the ears
  2. ii.The inner wrists
  3. iii.The inner elbows
  4. iv.Along the collarbone
III

The long wear

Small habits keep a signature with you all day.

  1. i.Begin on moisturised skin, after a shower or an unscented balm.
  2. ii.Let it settle and dry naturally on the skin.
  3. iii.Store the bottle somewhere cool, away from sun and humidity.
  4. iv.Refresh with a single light mist rather than several.
